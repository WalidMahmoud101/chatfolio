Chatfolio

Save the PDFs, photos and videos from your WhatsApp chats, sorted by chat and date, without duplicates.

Import any chat

Export a chat or group from WhatsApp and share it to the app. Choose PDFs, images, videos or all of them.

Smart file names

Transfer receipts and invoices are read on your iPhone: beneficiary or company, amount and date go into the file name.

Search inside files

Find any receipt by a name, an amount, a date or any word written in the document.

Whole chat pages

Keep a readable copy of every chat, with sender, time and the archived files.

Dropbox & Google Drive

Optionally upload to your own account. Duplicates are skipped and interrupted uploads resume.

Private

Everything is processed on your device. Optional Face ID lock. The developer receives no data.
