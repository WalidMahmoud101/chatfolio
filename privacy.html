3brother Archive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

3brother Archive does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and the developer receives nothing. The app has no account of its own, no analytics and no advertising. It connects to the internet only if you choose to upload files to your own Dropbox or Google Drive account.

What the app processes, and where

  • Files you choose: the WhatsApp chat exports, PDFs and images you import are read on your device only. The app saves copies of the PDFs and images into its own folder on your iPhone (visible in the Files app).
  • Text recognition: to name files, the app reads text inside PDFs and images (for example a beneficiary name, an amount and a transfer date) using Apple’s on-device text recognition. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Search index: the details and text read from your files are stored on your device so you can search them. They never leave the device.
  • Cloud copy (optional): if you turn on Dropbox or Google Drive and pick a folder, the app copies archived files into that folder on your iPhone. The Dropbox or Google Drive app then uploads them under its own privacy policy.
  • Signing in to Dropbox or Google Drive (optional): if you connect an account, the app uploads the files you choose directly to your account over an encrypted connection. Sign-in happens on Dropbox's or Google's own page; the app never sees your password. The sign-in token is kept only in the iPhone Keychain and is deleted when you disconnect. With Google Drive the app can access only the files and folders it created. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • Chat pages: when enabled, the text of an imported chat export is saved as an HTML page in the archive on your device.
  • Face ID: if you turn on the app lock, Face ID / Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app never receives or stores biometric data; it only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Data sharing

The app does not send data to the developer or to any third party. A file leaves your device only when you share it yourself (for example with the Share button).

Deleting your data

Delete files or the whole archive from inside the app, or delete them in the Files app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

سياسة الخصوصية (بالعربي)

تطبيق 3brother Archive لا يجمع أي بيانات شخصية ولا يبيعها أو يشاركها، والمطوّر لا يستلم أي شيء. كل المعالجة تتم على جهازك، والتطبيق يتصل بالإنترنت فقط لو اخترت رفع ملفات لحسابك على Dropbox أو Google Drive.

  • الملفات التي تختارها (تصدير شات واتساب، PDF، صور) تُقرأ على جهازك فقط، وتُحفظ نسخة منها داخل مجلد التطبيق على الآيفون.
  • قراءة اسم المستفيد والمبلغ والتاريخ من داخل الملفات تتم بخاصية التعرف على النص من Apple على الجهاز نفسه.
  • فهرس البحث محفوظ على جهازك فقط.
  • Face ID يتعامل معه نظام iOS بالكامل، والتطبيق لا يستلم أي بيانات بيومترية.
  • الرفع لـ Dropbox أو Google Drive اختياري: إما عن طريق التطبيق المتثبت على الآيفون، أو بتسجيل الدخول لحسابك من صفحتهم الرسمية. الباسورد مش بيوصل للتطبيق، والتوكن محفوظ في Keychain بس، والملفات بتروح لحسابك انت مباشرة.
  • حذف التطبيق يحذف كل بياناته من جهازك.
